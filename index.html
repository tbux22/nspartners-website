Trajah Buxton

Founder · New York

Nova Strategy Partners is led by Trajah Buxton, a New York-based real estate and distressed debt specialist. His background includes institutional advisory experience at Deloitte and hands-on valuation and due diligence work with Fortress Investment Group, Blackstone, and Centerbridge. From August 2024 to August 2025 he served as Director of the Economic Opportunity Hub at Open Doors, a nonprofit in Norwalk, Connecticut.
